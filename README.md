## About

I design and ship software at the intersection of blockchain and artificial intelligence. My work spans audited smart contracts and DeFi protocols on one side, and LLM-driven agents, retrieval systems, and data pipelines on the other — delivered as complete, production-ready products rather than prototypes.

I work with founders and teams from architecture through deployment, with an emphasis on correctness, security, and maintainability.

---

## What I Build

**Blockchain**
- DeFi protocols: automated market makers, staking, vaults, and yield strategies
- Token and NFT systems built on ERC-20, ERC-721, and ERC-1155 standards
- Trading infrastructure: DEX integrations, arbitrage and market-making bots
- On-chain analytics, indexing, and subgraph development
- Contract review, test coverage, and gas optimization

**Artificial Intelligence**
- Retrieval-augmented generation pipelines over private and on-chain data
- Autonomous agents with tool use, planning, and structured output
- LLM integration into existing products, including evaluation and guardrails
- Market analysis and signal generation for trading systems
- Model serving, inference APIs, and data preprocessing workflows
